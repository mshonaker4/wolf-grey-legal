QuickBooks disconnection

If you have completed disconnection in QuickBooks, that company connection has been revoked through Intuit. This page cannot independently check your connection status, and visiting it alone does not revoke access.

To disconnect, use QuickBooks' connected-app controls to remove Wolf Grey Context from the intended company and stop using the local connector for that connection. Verify completion in QuickBooks.

Disconnection does not delete existing local reports or snapshots. Handle local copies and backups separately as described in the Privacy Policy.

To authorize access again, follow the connect or reconnect instructions in your local application.

For help, contact formosavacation@gmail.com. Do not include tokens, credentials or financial records.
