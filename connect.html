Connect or reconnect QuickBooks

This internal application starts authorization on your computer. Visiting this page does not connect a company.

  1. Open your installed Wolf Grey Context application on your computer. For first-time setup, enter the production app credentials and its exact registered redirect URI in the application's private local configuration prompts.
  2. Start the application's authorization command and open its generated Intuit consent link in your browser.
  3. Sign in to Intuit and select the company you are authorized to connect.
  4. Copy the full redirected address from the browser address bar into the application's hidden completion prompt within ten minutes. Keep that address private; do not send it by email or paste it into chat.
  5. Verify the company identity in the local application before requesting reports.

To reconnect an existing installation, repeat the authorization and completion steps using that installation's existing configuration. Do not overwrite the connection file or exchange the same authorization code in another tool.

If you arrive here from QuickBooks, return to the local application to start these steps. For help, contact formosavacation@gmail.com without including credentials or callback addresses.
