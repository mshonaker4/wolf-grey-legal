Wolf Grey Context

Internal, owner-operated QuickBooks reporting tool

Wolf Grey Context retrieves company information, account and class lists, Profit and Loss reports and Balance Sheets on demand. Its connector runs on the authorized operator's computer and stores records locally for evidence review and report comparisons.

This website is the application's public information and launch page. It does not display accounting records or store application credentials. After authorizing through Intuit, return to the local application to finish the connection and request reports.
